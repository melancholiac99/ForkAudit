# Exploratory result: E4 credit predictability audit — 2026-11-12

**Source:** existing E4 archived five-arm executed replays; no new GPU generation or policy training. **Status:** post-hoc screening result, **not** a pre-registered confirmation of the 2026-11-12 theoretical proposal.

Reproducible run: https://github.com/melancholiac99/ForkAudit/actions/runs/43161948908
Full outputs (summary.json, REPORT.md, points.csv, replicate_holdout.csv): https://github.com/melancholiac99/ForkAudit/actions/runs/43161948908/artifacts/13918222274

## Design

- E4: 6,016 defined decision points, 308 distinct games represented in these points; 5 arms each and 3 independent binary continuation rewards per arm.
- Five-fold **game-held-out** regression predicting each arm's three-run mean terminal success from only the Student's original prompt and that candidate action. TF-IDF + fixed Ridge(alpha=10). The action-only input is a negative control.
- Group observed best-minus-worst replay gaps with ties preserved. Because each arm has just 3 binary continuations, there are only three *nonzero* gap levels: 1/3, 2/3, and 1.
- Rotate one continuation out for independent evaluation: select arms on the other two, then measure the held-out one. This diagnoses Monte Carlo selection bias but not hidden-state aliasing.

## Results

- **4,430 / 6,016 (73.64%)** have all five arm means tied (observed gap 0). The remaining **1,586** have nonzero gaps.
- Out-of-fold value MSE is **0.03961**, versus a fold-training-mean baseline of **0.04504** (relative R² **0.121**). Action-only MSE is **0.04312**. The student-visible prompt contributes some forecast signal beyond the action alone, but the text model is imperfect.
- The mean **signed predicted** value difference for the measured top-minus-bottom actions *increases* with measured gap:

| Measured gap | Points | Mean predicted top-minus-bottom | Mean positive clipped predicted/observed ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1/3 | 894 | +0.0050 | 0.0431 |
| 2/3 | 462 | +0.0141 | 0.0352 |
| 1 | 230 | +0.0337 | 0.0390 |

- Game-cluster bootstrap (500 resamples) Spearman for gap vs **clipped ratio**: **+0.091**, 95% CI **[+0.026, +0.158]**. Spearman for gap vs **absolute predicted difference**: **+0.201**, 95% CI **[+0.138, +0.266]**.
- When arms are selected on 2 rollouts and evaluated on the held-out third:

| Selection gap | Selected comparisons | Mean independently observed gap |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5 | 2,818 | +0.0529 |
| 1.0 | 1,329 | +0.2792 |

These held-out values are **much smaller** than the selection gaps, confirming substantial winner's-curse / rollout sampling noise. But larger selection gaps also have larger independent follow-up gaps, so the measured difference is not purely sampling noise.

## What the results do and do not say

1. **The proposed strong monotone prediction ("the bigger the replay gap, the lower the predictable proportion") is not supported by this E4 screening test.** The three clipped-ratio bin means are nearly flat, not steadily declining; the point-level rank correlation is slightly positive. Absolute predicted differences rise.
2. Large measured gaps are still **mostly not reproduced in the ridge model's prompt-conditional predictions**. However, this cannot be interpreted as a measured "96% luck fraction" or as proof of Student informational impossibility: shrinkage, limited text features, sparse rewards and only three Monte Carlo outcomes per arm all matter.
3. The independent-replicate check exposes **large selection noise**. This is a competing explanation of why expensive five-arm labels may underperform; it must be separated from hidden-state luck before telling a causal story.
4. The experiment does **not** identify EVPI, does not compare matching hidden states conditioned on exactly the same Student information, and does not train projected-credit DPO. It cannot establish the claimed general failure of non-linear credit selection.

**Recommended inference:** the zero-GPU diagnostic was worth running, but does **not** validate the "decisive steps teach the least" paper premise. To pursue that claim, use more reliable held-out replay banks (e.g. original M0 replay banks if restored), stronger prompt-only predictors / sensitivity to regularization, and controlled observability experiments before designing EVPI-based training rewards.

Frozen E4 rules, original data and prior reports were not modified.
